What kind of game is Magic?

A card game usually played one-on-one. The best part is building your own deck.

⚔️Usually one-on-one

Both start at 20 life; bring your opponent to 0 and you win. There are also team formats for pairs, and formats for three or more players.

🃏You build the deck

Pick the cards you like and put together a deck that is yours.

🌍30+ years and still going

Created in 1993, with tournaments held all over the world.

One turn at a glance

  1. UntapReady the cards you used last turn
  2. DrawDraw one card
  3. PlayPlay a land, then spend mana on spells and creatures
  4. AttackAttack with your creatures
  5. EndPass the turn

Lands produce mana, the energy you spend on spells. You can play only one land per turn, so you can cast bigger cards as the game goes on.

Five colors, five ways to play

Cards come in five colors, and each color is good at different things. Most decks use one or two.

White
Order and protection

Many small allies, flying creatures and life gain make for a sturdy game.

Blue
Knowledge and control

Counter spells, draw cards and fly over the board.

Black
Sacrifice and removal

Lots of ways to destroy creatures, often at a cost to you.

Red
Speed and fire

Damage spells that hit creatures or your opponent directly.

Green
Size and growth

Big creatures and extra mana. Win by being bigger.

There are also colorless cards (artifacts, for example) that fit in any deck.

Card types

These seven cover most of what you will see. The images are examples for illustration and may not be playable in Arena today.

Serra Angel
CreatureSerra Angel

A being that fights on the battlefield. It attacks, and it blocks.

Lightning Bolt
InstantLightning Bolt

A spell you can cast almost any time, even on your opponent's turn. It is used up once cast.

Wrath of God
SorceryWrath of God

A spell you can cast only on your own turn, in your main phase. Often big effects.

Pacifism
EnchantmentPacifism

An effect that stays on the battlefield. This one stops a creature from fighting.

Sol Ring
ArtifactSol Ring

A permanent like a tool. Many are colorless and go in any deck.

Chandra, Torch of Defiance
PlaneswalkerChandra, Torch of Defiance

A powerful mage on your side. Use an ability each turn; attacks wear her down.

Forest
LandForest

Makes mana — the foundation of your deck. About 40% of a deck is lands.

How to read a card

Once you know the numbers and symbols, you can judge a card you have never seen.

  1. 1Name
  2. 2Mana cost (what you pay to cast it)
  3. 3Art
  4. 4Type (Creature, etc.)
  5. 5Rules text (abilities)
  6. 6Power / Toughness (attack / health)

Reading the mana cost

Serra Angel costs : two white mana plus three mana of any color, five in total. A number means "any color", a colored symbol means mana of that color from your lands.

4/4 means it deals 4 damage and is destroyed by 4 damage.

What is a format?

A format is a set of rules on which cards you may use and how many cards the deck holds. There are two big families: Constructed and Limited.

ConstructedLimited
Your cardsAnything in your collectionOnly the packs you open on the spot
Deck size60+ cards (max 4 copies of a card)40+ cards
PreparationYou need to collect cardsJust the entry fee; everyone starts equal
On ArenaStandard, Explorer, Historic, etc.Draft, Sealed
Good if youWant to research and refine strong decksWant to start now without collecting

There are also formats with a different number of players, such as Two-Headed Giant (pairs share 30 life) and Commander (three or more). Limited on Arena, though, is one-on-one.

Both are fun. This site focuses on Limited.

Glossary
Mana
The energy you spend on spells, produced by lands.
Spell
Any card you cast from hand. Creatures are spells too.
Battlefield
Where cards are played and fight.
Graveyard
Where used or destroyed cards go.
Tap
Turning a card sideways. Using a land taps it.
Life
Your health. You start at 20; at 0 you lose.
Block
Stopping an attacker with one of your creatures.
Flying
Can't be blocked by creatures without flying.
