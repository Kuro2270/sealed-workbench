What is Limited?

You build a deck on the spot, using only the cards from packs you open. Everyone starts on equal footing, so a bigger collection does not decide the game.

Sealed

Open six packs at once and build a 40-card deck from that pool.

  • Build calmly, on your own
  • Deck-building is the skill
  • An easier way to start than Draft

Draft

Eight players sit in a pod, take one card from a pack and pass it on (three packs, 42 picks).

  • Tests what you choose to take
  • Reading the others is half the fun
  • Includes building the deck too

What matters in Limited　It is not only about collecting strong cards; the balance of all 40 decides games. The guide below keeps you from guessing.

Quick Draft and Premier Draft

MTG Arena has two kinds of Draft. Either way, your opponents in games are real players.

Quick DraftPremier Draft
You draft withComputer playersReal players (8 including you)
Pick timerNone (go at your own pace)Yes, for each pick
Entry feeAbout half of Premier DraftHigher (bigger prizes too)
WhenSets rotate by periodRight after a new set's release

If you're new to Draft, start with Quick Draft.

Event schedule

Limited on Arena isn't always the same set. Events follow the official event schedule.

📦Premier Draft and Sealed

Start with the new set as soon as it comes out.

🔄Quick Draft

Rotates through sets by period, so older sets come around too, not just the newest one.

📅How often new sets come out

Roughly every six to eight weeks. The gap varies from set to set, so check the official event schedule for details.

When unsure, start from this shape.

A rough guide: 16–17 lands, 15–17 creatures, and 6–8 spells such as removal and tricks. Many current guides use 17 lands, 17 creatures and 6 spells as the baseline (aggressive decks may drop to 16 lands).

If you only play big, powerful cards, you'll have nothing to cast in the early and middle game, and you'll lose. Spreading out your cards' mana costs is called the mana curve: lots of cheap cards, fewer expensive ones.

🎨Two colors at most

Split your lands to match, for example 9 Plains and 8 Islands.

⚖️Enough creatures

Too few and you struggle both to attack and to defend.

🎯Don't forget removal

With few spell slots, choose your removal carefully. Creatures that act as removal count too.

Why removal is so strong in Limited

Removal is any card that destroys a creature or stops it from fighting.

Why it is strong

①One card answers their best

Opponents will play big or flying creatures. Removal deals with them with one card.

②There is only a little of it

A pack has just a few removal spells. In Draft, taking them early makes a difference.

③It is a good trade

One card of yours for one strong card of theirs swings the board your way.

Checking which cards are strong

The set reports on this site show card win rates by color and rarity from real game data. Look at GIH WR (win rate in games where the card was in hand or on the battlefield); about 3 points above the set average is a strong card.
