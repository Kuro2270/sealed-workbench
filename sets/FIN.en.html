Draft 864,813 games · overall win rate 56.6%

Built 2026-10-14

Final Fantasy Draft on MTG Arena: 864,813 games, 56.6% overall win rate. On speed, fast decks clearly win more. White (56.7%) and Blue (56.4%) are the strongest colours; the best two-colour pair is Blue/Red at 58.2%, and the weakest is Black/Red at 55.9%. By card, the top common is White Auracite, the top uncommon is Dragoon's Lance, the top rare is Ardyn, the Usurper (game-in-hand win rate).

Format speed

A fast format. Decks weighted toward cheap spells clearly win more.Decks averaging under 2.9 mana value have the highest win rate, at 58.1%. Games last 9.2 turns on average, about the same as the last 8 sets.

About Limited card ratings

What is a Limited card rating?

A number that says how strong a card is. Ratings given by people include personal taste, so this page uses win rates from real games instead.

How should I read the GIH win rate?

A card above the set's overall win rate was in decks that won more often. Cards with few games swing a lot, and cards that go into strong decks tend to look better than they are, so treat it as a guide and read it together with colours and synergy.

When are the ratings updated?

Every Monday (Japan time), from the latest 17Lands data.
