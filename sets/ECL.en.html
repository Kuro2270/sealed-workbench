Real win rates for Magic: The Gathering Arena Draft and Sealed, aggregated from 17Lands public data.　Released 2026-01-23

Draft 593,444 games · overall win rate 55.6%

Built 2026-09-18

Lorwyn Eclipsed Draft on MTG Arena: 593,444 games, 55.6% overall win rate. On speed, speed makes little difference. Green (55.9%) and White (55.8%) are the strongest colours; the best two-colour pair is Black/Green at 57.5%, and the weakest is Black/Red at 54.1%. By card, the top common is Dawnhand Eulogist, the top uncommon is Deepchannel Duelist, the top rare is Ashling's Command (game-in-hand win rate).

Format speed

Speed does not decide much here. What goes in the deck matters more.Decks averaging under 2.9 mana value have the highest win rate, at 56.0%. Games last 9.4 turns on average, on the long side of the last 8 sets.

By card, the top common is Mistmeadow Council, the top uncommon is Thoughtweft Lieutenant, the top rare is Sunderflock (game-in-hand win rate).

Format speed
