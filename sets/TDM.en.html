Tarkir: Dragonstorm — Limited format report TDM

Real win rates for Magic: The Gathering Arena Draft and Sealed, aggregated from 17Lands public data.　Released 2025-04-11

Draft 617,527 games · overall win rate 55.6%

Built 2026-09-18

Tarkir: Dragonstorm Draft on MTG Arena: 617,527 games, 55.6% overall win rate. On speed, speed makes little difference. Red (56.0%) and White (55.7%) are the strongest colours; the best two-colour pair is White/Red at 58.9%, and the weakest is Blue/Green at 55.4%. By card, the top common is Sibsig Appraiser, the top uncommon is Sonic Shrieker, the top rare is Marang River Regent (game-in-hand win rate).

Format speed

Speed does not decide much here. What goes in the deck matters more.Decks averaging under 2.9 mana value have the highest win rate, at 56.6%. Games last 9.1 turns on average, about the same as the last 8 sets.

Built 2026-09-18

Format speed

A slow format. Decks that can support expensive cards win more.Decks averaging 3.5 mana value or more have the highest win rate, at 57.9%. Games last 9.3 turns on average, on the long side of the last 8 sets.
