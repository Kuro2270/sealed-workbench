About Limited card ratings

What is a Limited card rating?

A number that says how strong a card is. Ratings given by people include personal taste, so this page uses win rates from real games instead.

How should I read the GIH win rate?

A card above the set's overall win rate was in decks that won more often. Cards with few games swing a lot, and cards that go into strong decks tend to look better than they are, so treat it as a guide and read it together with colours and synergy.

When are the ratings updated?

Every Monday (Japan time), from the latest 17Lands data.
