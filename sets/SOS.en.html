Secrets of Strixhaven — Limited format report SOS

Real win rates for Magic: The Gathering Arena Draft and Sealed, aggregated from 17Lands public data.　Released 2026-04-24

Draft 646,900 games · overall win rate 55.9%

Built 2026-10-03

Secrets of Strixhaven Draft on MTG Arena: 646,900 games, 55.9% overall win rate. On speed, speed makes little difference. White (57.4%) and Black (55.9%) are the strongest colours; the best two-colour pair is White/Black at 58.2%, and the weakest is Blue/Red at 55.0%. By card, the top common is Elite Interceptor, the top uncommon is Snarl Song, the top rare is Together as One (game-in-hand win rate).
