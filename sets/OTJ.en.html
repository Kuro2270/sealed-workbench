Real win rates for Magic: The Gathering Arena Draft and Sealed, aggregated from 17Lands public data.　Released 2024-04-19

Draft 1,388,295 games · overall win rate 54.8%

Built 2026-10-02

Outlaws of Thunder Junction Draft on MTG Arena: 1,388,295 games, 54.8% overall win rate. On speed, it leans slow — heavier decks win a little more. Green (55.7%) and White (55.5%) are the strongest colours; the best two-colour pair is White/Green at 57.8%, and the weakest is Black/Red at 53.5%. By card, the top common is Throw from the Saddle, the top uncommon is Outcaster Greenblade, the top rare is Bonny Pall, Clearcutter (game-in-hand win rate).

Format speed

Decks averaging 3.5 mana value or more have the highest win rate, at 56.2%. Compared with the last 8 sets, the pace is about the same.
